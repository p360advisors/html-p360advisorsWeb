Advisory services

Where we can help

Bring in experienced financial and operational guidance when you need it, with an advisory scope shaped around your business.

Financial management & profitability

Review financial statements, forecast cash flow, build budgets and identify opportunities to manage expenses, improve margins and strengthen working capital.

Operations & efficiency

Assess your operating model, find process bottlenecks, measure productivity and establish procedures that make day-to-day work more consistent.

Access to capital & lending readiness

Evaluate SBA and conventional financing options, review debt structure and credit readiness, and organize the financial documents lenders will ask for.

Leadership & people

Address employee turnover, clarify roles, strengthen onboarding and mentoring, and coach managers on performance, sales and service.

Business development & growth

Build a strategic plan, improve customer acquisition and retention, evaluate expansion opportunities and track the KPIs that matter.

Banking & treasury management

Review banking relationships, fees and merchant processing costs. Improve cash management, payment workflows and collections.

Our approach

The Business360 assessment

We look across six parts of your business to find what is driving performance and where a change can make the most difference. The assessment gives us a baseline for setting priorities and tracking progress.

  1. Assess the business. Review financials, operations and your goals as an owner.
  2. Agree on priorities. Turn findings into a focused action plan with milestones.
  3. Track progress. Review measures such as margins, cash flow, retention and financing readiness.

What we assess

  • Financial health
  • Operational efficiency
  • People and leadership
  • Sales and growth
  • Capital readiness
  • Strategic direction

Who we serve

Established businesses ready for their next step

Our typical clients have been operating for at least two years, generate $500,000–$20 million in annual revenue and employ 5–100 people. They often need experienced guidance on cash flow, profitability, productivity or expansion and have limited in-house advisory resources.

We work with owners across construction, healthcare practices, restaurants, professional services, distribution, logistics, manufacturing, automotive and family-owned businesses.

Referral partners

Bankers, SBA lenders, CPAs, business attorneys, commercial real estate professionals, HR and payroll providers, and local business associations can direct owners to P360 for operational guidance and financing preparation.
